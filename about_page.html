AWARDS

HONORABLE MENTION — 24TH AMS POSTER GALLERY COMPETITION

WYGODNIE I ODPOWIEDZIALNE,
CZYLI JAK?

(COMFORTABLE AND RESPONSIBLE – BUT HOW?)

I have received an Honorable Mention in the 24th AMS Poster Gallery Competition, a nationwide poster design competition in Poland. The brief focused on encouraging people to think about how convenience and responsibility can go hand in hand in everyday life through visual communication.

I created a poster featuring a girl wearing sunglasses made of solar panels as a visual metaphor for combining comfort with environmental responsibility. I wanted to show that sustainable solutions, like renewable energy, don't have to feel like a sacrifice, they can become a natural part of our everyday lives. I kept the design simple and visually striking, letting the idea speak for itself while encouraging people to think about how even small choices can have a positive impact.

Following the competition, my poster was displayed at bus stops in cities across Poland and featured in several exhibitions, including one held at the historic Royal Łazienki Park in Warsaw.
